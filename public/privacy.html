Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Utah Remedy is a service of Quazar16 LLC. We collect as little as we can. Here’s what that means today.

What we collect now

  • Age check: you enter your date of birth to pass the 21+ check. We use it once to check your age and do not store it: birthdates entered at the age gate are never saved or logged. We then set a cookie that only records whether you passed, for 30 days. If you are under 21, a short-lived cookie (24 hours) remembers that too.
  • Basic technical logs: our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Visit counts: we count anonymous visits using a daily-changing code that can’t identify you, and we don’t store IP addresses or birthdates.
  • Feedback form: if you send us a message through the feedback form, we save what you type: the topic, your message, and the optional city, link, and email fields. Your email is optional, and we use it only to reply to that message. We don’t send any confirmation email or add you to any list. Saved messages aren’t deleted automatically.
  • Spam protection on the feedback form: to block spam, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser and IP address. We also limit each connection to 5 messages per hour. For that limit, we keep a one-way scrambled code made from your IP address, not the address itself. It counts only for one hour, and older entries are deleted the next time the limit is checked. If a message is filtered as spam, we keep a short sample of it (the first 200 characters, the topic, the reason, and that same scrambled code) for 14 days. Older samples are deleted the next time another message is filtered. The scrambled code is never saved with messages that get through.
  • Account and sign-in: if you sign in to use watches, we save your email address, the date you created your account, when you confirmed you are 21 or older, and which version of our Terms you agreed to. We sign you in with a one-time link sent to your email; there are no passwords. Each link works once, expires after 15 minutes, and we keep only a scrambled copy of it, deleted after 24 hours. Before you use a link, we keep the email address you typed with that pending link for up to 24 hours; if you never use it, no account is created.
  • Watches and alerts: we save the bottles you watch (up to 5), any stores you pick, and a record of the alerts we sent or skipped for each watch (bottle, stores, date, and whether the email was sent). We use your email only for sign-in links and the stock alerts you asked for. We don’t send newsletters or marketing, and we don’t add you to any other list.
  • Sign-in protection: the sign-in form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser and IP address. To limit repeated requests, we keep one-way scrambled codes made from your IP address and from the email address entered, not the addresses themselves, for 24 hours.

We don’t use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party trackers. The only measurement we do is the anonymous daily-code visit count described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

Alert records are deleted after 90 days. Signed-in sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out. If you don’t sign in for 12 months and have no active watches, we delete your account. You can delete your account at any time from the Account page: this removes your email, watches, alert records, and sessions right away. Any feedback you sent stays, but is no longer linked to your account. Every alert email has a link to stop alerts for that bottle or for all bottles, with no sign-in needed. You can also email hello@utahremedy.com to ask what we hold about you or to delete it.

Later

We email you only what you asked for: sign-in links and alerts for bottles you watch. Emails are sent through Cloudflare, our hosting provider. If we ever want to collect anything else, we’ll update this policy first and explain what and why.

Tips

Optional “Tip the dev” links and QR codes go to Cash App and Venmo, which are third-party services with their own privacy policies. We don’t process payments, and no payment details are entered on or sent through this site.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t share it with DABS or other third parties except service providers that help run the site, or where the law requires it.
  • We don’t share your email or watches with DABS, stores, or anyone else, other than the service providers that run the site (Cloudflare).

Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies: the age-check cookies described above; if you sign in, a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days and is removed when you sign out; and on the feedback, sign-in, and account forms, short-lived security cookies (up to 2 hours) that protect against forged submissions. You can clear them in your browser at any time. You’ll just see the age check again and need to sign in again.

Children

Utah Remedy is not for anyone under 21, and we don’t knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

We’ll post any updates here and change the “last updated” date.
