The remedy for Utah liquor hunting.

Allocated drops, shelf checks, special orders. In Utah, finding a particular bottle can take a lot of legwork. Utah Remedy puts the public state-store data and fresh reports from fellow hunters in one place, so you spend less time driving around and more time enjoying what you found.

Search is in beta. Browse stores. Counts come from public DABS data and can lag; verify with the store before you go.

Built straight
